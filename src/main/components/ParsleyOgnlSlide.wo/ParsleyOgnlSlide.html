parsley-ognl

For those who actually need OGNL expressions

pom.xml

<dependency>
	<groupId>is.rebbi.parsley</groupId>
	<artifactId>parsley-ognl</artifactId>
</dependency>

Application.java

Parsley.configure()
	.associationFactory(
		new ParsleyOgnlAssociationFactory() )
	.register();

A ~ makes a binding an OGNL expression

<wo:str value="~price * quantity" />
<wo:if condition="~items.size() > 0">…</wo:if>
  • An optional module: OGNL isn't on the classpath unless you add it
  • Every other binding works as before: $keypath, constants, $!negation
