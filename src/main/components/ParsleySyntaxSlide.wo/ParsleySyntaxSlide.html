What's new in the syntax

HTML comments are just content

<!-- rendered, like any other HTML -->

p:comment: never rendered

<p:comment>
	Not in the page, and not run:
	<wo:str value="$this.wont.execute" />
</p:comment>

p:raw: passed through untouched

<p:raw>
<script>
	var template = "<wo:SomeComponent />";
</script>
</p:raw>
