Resources

Resources and their URLs work exactly the same in development and in production

Served by the application

GET /res/app/css/site@3f9c1e07ab.css

Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable
ETag: "3f9c1e07ab"
  • Nothing to copy to the web server
  • Stamped with its content: cached for good, fresh after a deploy
  • An ETag on every resource: unchanged, it's a 304 Not Modified with no body
  • Ranges, so video and audio seek

Public resources, at the root

routes.fallback( new ERXPublicResources() );
src/main/webserver-resources/public/
    robots.txt          → /robots.txt
    favicon.ico         → /favicon.ico
    .well-known/…       → /.well-known/…
  • Asked of when no route answers
