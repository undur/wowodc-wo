The .woa, simplified

Before

MyApp.woa/
    MyApp
    MyApp.cmd
    Contents/
        Info.plist
        MacOS/
            MyApp
            MacOSClassPath.txt
            MacOSXServerClassPath.txt
        UNIX/
            UNIXClassPath.txt
        Windows/
            MyApp.cmd
            CLSSPATH.TXT
            SUBPATHS.TXT
        Resources/
            Java/
        WebServerResources/

After

MyApp.woa/
    MyApp           the launch script
    config.txt      how to launch
    classpath.txt   one entry per line
    Contents/
        Info.plist
        Resources/
            Java/
        WebServerResources/

config.txt

principalClass=myapp.Application
jvm=/opt/jdk-25/bin/java
jvmOptions=-Xmx2g
  • No per-platform folders and classpath files
  • Plain text you can read, and change on the server
